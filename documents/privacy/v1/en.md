# Tanks Defense Privacy Policy

**Version 1**  
**Effective date: August 19, 2026**

## 1. General Provisions

1.1. This Privacy Policy describes how Vadim Flyurovich Zinnatullin, who develops and publishes Tanks Defense under the name Green Box Team (the "Controller"), processes data relating to Tanks Defense users (each a "User").

1.2. This Policy applies to the Tanks Defense game, the `tanksdefense.com` website, game accounts ("Accounts"), servers, and related online features (collectively, the "Service"). It applies whether the User uses an Account or plays the single-player mode available without an Account.

1.3. This Policy explains what data is processed when the Service is used, the purposes and legal bases for processing, who may receive the data, how it is retained, and how the User may exercise their rights.

1.4. The Service may be used only by persons who are at least 13 years old. If applicable law requires the involvement or consent of a parent or other legal guardian, a minor may use the Service only if that requirement is satisfied.

1.5. When first launching the game, the User confirms that they have read this Policy. Acknowledging this Policy does not by itself replace separate consent to data processing where such consent is required by applicable law.

1.6. Use of the Service is governed by the [Terms of Service](https://tanksdefense.com/terms), and purchases of paid Virtual Content are governed by the [Purchase Terms](https://tanksdefense.com/offer).

## 2. Data We Process, Why We Process It, and Legal Bases

2.1. The Controller processes the following categories of data to the extent necessary for the purposes described below:

- **Account data** — email address, sign-in method information, Account identifiers, and authentication tokens; used to create an Account, sign in, recover access, and protect the Account. When signing in by email or through Google, the Service database stores a hash of the email address rather than the email address in plain text;
- **profile and game data** — game ID, nickname, profile customization, settings, progress, statistics, inventory, Crystals, Premium, cloud saves, and information about participation in matches; used to save progress and provide game features;
- **social feature data** — friends, messages, comments, reactions, groups, reports, and related moderation decisions; used for communication between users, community features, review of reports, and enforcement of the game rules;
- **technical data** — IP address and its hash, installation and client identifiers, information about the device, operating system, game version and language, connection, sessions, and errors; used for server communication, compatibility, diagnostics, failure prevention, and protection of the Service;
- **game analytics and security data** — game usage events, session duration, performance, connection latency, and information about possible violations or interference with the game; used to develop Tanks Defense, correct errors, ensure fair play, and prevent abuse;
- **purchase data** — game IDs of the Buyer and Recipient, selected offer, amount, currency, transaction number, time and status, and the Virtual Content credited; used to place and complete purchases, issue refunds, provide support, and comply with legal requirements;
- **support request data** — sender address, contents of the request, and any attached materials voluntarily provided by the User to support; used to respond and resolve the matter described in the request;
- **push notification identifier** — a random technical ID associated with the Account; used to deliver notifications if the User has allowed them.

2.2. The Controller does not receive or store full payment card details. Those details are processed by the payment provider. The Controller also does not request users' passport details, biometric data, precise geolocation, or health information.

2.3. A diagnostic report containing technical information and the game log is transmitted to the Controller only after the User chooses to send it through the relevant feature.

2.4. The website does not use third-party web analytics and stores in the browser only the information necessary for authentication and operation of its features. The Service does not alter its data processing practices in response to a `Do Not Track` signal sent by the browser. Processing by external services is described in Section 3 of this Policy.

2.5. Depending on the purpose and applicable law, data is processed to provide the Service and perform agreements with the User, comply with the Controller's legal obligations, protect the legitimate interests of the Controller and users, or on the basis of consent where consent is required. Legitimate interests include maintaining the operation and security of the Service, preventing fraud and violations, correcting errors, and reasonably developing Tanks Defense while respecting the User's rights.

## 3. Sharing Data with Third Parties

3.1. The Controller shares with external services only the data necessary to perform the relevant function:

- [Google](https://policies.google.com/privacy?hl=en-US) processes data necessary for the User's selected sign-in method using a Google Account;
- [Robokassa](https://robokassa.com/privacy-policy/) receives information necessary to process payments, generate receipts, and issue refunds. The User provides payment details directly to the payment provider;
- [OneSignal](https://onesignal.com/privacy_policy) processes a random identifier, device and subscription information, and the contents of a notification in order to deliver push notifications;
- [Yandex](https://yandex.com/legal/confidential/en/?lang=en) may process technical information about the device, network, ad impressions, and interactions with ads in order to display advertising. For users in regions where the GDPR applies, data collection for analytics and ad targeting is not enabled;
- [Photon](https://www.photonengine.com/terms/privacyandcookiepolicy) processes the game ID, nickname, IP address, connection and room information, and game events in order to run multiplayer matches;
- [OpenAI](https://openai.com/policies/row-privacy-policy/) receives only the nickname proposed by the User in order to automatically review it before it is used in the Service.

3.2. Data may also be processed by providers of email, server, and other technical services to the extent necessary to send sign-in codes, operate the website and servers, create backups, and protect the Service.

3.3. Depending on the nature of their services, external services process data on the Controller's behalf or independently under their own policies. Some of them are located, or use servers located, outside the User's country, so data may be processed in other countries. Where the GDPR applies to such a transfer, it is made, depending on the recipient, on the basis of an adequacy decision, Standard Contractual Clauses, or another transfer mechanism permitted by the GDPR. Information about the safeguards used may be requested through support.

3.4. The Controller may disclose data to a government authority, court, or other authorized person where disclosure is required by law, or where necessary to protect the rights and safety of the Controller, Users, or third parties to the extent permitted by law.

## 4. Data Retention, User Rights, and Account Deletion

4.1. Data is retained no longer than necessary to operate the Service and the Account, fulfill the purposes described in this Policy, maintain security, resolve disputes, and comply with legal requirements. The retention period depends on the category of data and the circumstances of its processing.

4.2. Once the relevant purpose has been fulfilled, data is deleted, de-identified, or retained in a limited form where necessary to verify purchases, prevent fraud and circumvention of restrictions applied to an Account, protect the parties' rights, or comply with a legal obligation. Deleted data may temporarily remain in backups until they are replaced in the ordinary course.

4.3. The User may ask what data is associated with their Account, obtain a copy, request correction of inaccurate information, or request deletion of the data and Account. Where processing is based on consent, the User may withdraw that consent. Other rights, including restriction of or objection to processing and the right to lodge a complaint with a competent supervisory authority, may be exercised where provided by applicable law.

4.4. To exercise their rights, the User may contact the Controller through any method listed on the [support page](https://tanksdefense.com/support). The Controller may request information necessary to verify the User's identity and protect the Account from unauthorized access, and will respond within the period required by applicable law.

4.5. Instructions for deleting an Account are available on the [Account deletion page](https://tanksdefense.com/account-deletion). The User sends a request to the support email address, after which the moderation team verifies ownership of the Account and processes the request.

4.6. When an Account is deleted, access to it is terminated, authentication data, the cloud save, and the associated push notification identifier are deleted, and the profile and User Content are deleted or de-identified. Records concerning purchases, matches, enforcement actions, violations, and other matters may be retained to the extent necessary for the purposes described in Section 4.2. Account deletion cannot be reversed and may result in the permanent loss of progress and Virtual Content.

4.7. Data stored directly on the User's device may remain after Account deletion until the User deletes the application data or the application itself. Support correspondence is retained as needed and may be deleted on request unless it must be retained to review the request, resolve a dispute, or comply with the law.

## 5. Data Security

5.1. The Controller takes reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect data, taking into account its nature and the potential risks. These measures include secure data transmission, access controls, hashing of certain identifiers and tokens, backups, logging, and monitoring of suspicious activity.

5.2. Access to data is limited to the Controller, persons authorized by the Controller, and the external services identified or described in Section 3 of this Policy. Each receives access only to the extent necessary to perform the relevant tasks.

5.3. No method of storing or transmitting data is completely secure. The Controller therefore does not guarantee the complete absence of failures, unauthorized access, or other incidents, but takes reasonable measures to prevent them and mitigate their consequences.

5.4. If the Controller becomes aware of an incident affecting user data, the Controller assesses its consequences, takes necessary measures, and notifies Users or competent authorities where and as required by applicable law.
